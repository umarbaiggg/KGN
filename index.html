Our Heritage & Promise

Hospitality Rooted in Sincerity & Purity

Founded by Umar in Johannesburg, South Africa, KGN (Khawaja Gareeb Nawaz) was established with one clear mission: to serve authentic, deeply flavorful South Asian cuisine and freshly prepared fast food in an environment of spotless hygiene and warm generosity.

Every spice blend is freshly ground in-house, every cut of meat is 100% Halaal-certified and inspected daily, and every order at KGN Takeaway – China Mall is cooked to order so you enjoy piping-hot perfection without the wait.
